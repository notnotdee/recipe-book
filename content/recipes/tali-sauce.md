## Ingredients

##### Yield: About 1¼ cups.

| Unit | Item |
| ---- | ---- |
| ⅓ cup | Chickpeas, drained and rinsed |
| ¼ cup | Nutritional yeast |
| 2 tbsp | Tahini | 
| 3 tbsp | EVOO |
| 2 tbsp | Fresh lemon juice | 
| ¼ cup | Cold water, plus more as needed | 
| 2 ea | Medium garlic cloves, roasted | 
| ½ ea | Small garlic cloves, raw | 
| ½ tsp | Dry mustard | 
| ¼ tsp | Ground cumin | 
| ¼ tsp | Turmeric | 
| ¼ tsp | Onion powder | 
| tt | Cayenne | 
| ½ tsp | Salt, or to taste |

## Preparation

Roast the garlic. Leave the cloves in their skins, rub with a drop of oil, and bake at 375°F for about 18 minutes, until soft. Cool and squeeze them out.

#### Step 1

Blend the chickpeas, nutritional yeast, tahini, lemon, water, roasted garlic, raw garlic, mustard, spices, and salt until completely smooth.

#### Step 2

With the blender running, slowly pour in the olive oil. Blend another 30–60 seconds.

#### Step 3

Add cold water 1 tablespoon at a time until it pours in a thick ribbon. It should be thinner than hummus but substantially thicker than salad dressing.

#### Step 4

Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes—ideally several hours. Then taste it on warm rice, not straight from the blender.

#### Step 5: Adjustments

Too lemony: Add 1 tablespoon chickpeas, 1 tablespoon nutritional yeast, and a splash of water.

Too flat or bland: It almost certainly needs more salt—add it a pinch at a time.

Too much like hummus: Add another tablespoon oil and enough water to loosen it.

Too sharp/raw: Leave it overnight; raw garlic softens considerably.

Missing richness: Add another teaspoon of tahini.

Bitter: Your olive oil may be too assertive. Replace half of it with avocado, grapeseed, or another neutral oil next time.
