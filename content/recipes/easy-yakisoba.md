## Ingredients

##### Yield:

| Unit | Item          |
| ---- | ------------- |
| 1 ea | Broccoli      |
| 1 ea | Red cabbage   |
| 1 ea | Shiitake      |
| 1 ea | Tempeh        |
| 1 ea | Fresh noodles |
| 5 ea | Garlic clove  |

## Preparation

#### Step 1

- Cook crushed garlic cloves in oil. When cooked, strain out garlic and return oil to pan
- Brown tempeh in garlicky oil; remove from pan
- Julienne red cabbage and leave to crisp in a bowl of cold, salted water
- Saute mushrooms
- Steam broccoli florets

#### Step 2

- Stir-fry noodles, then toss everything back together
